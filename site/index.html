EU AI Act: verifiable legal graph

A deterministic, open-source provision tree of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) in English and German, with a diff between the original and the 2026 consolidated text. It checks whether a quotation really appears in the Act, where, in which version, and whether it applies on a given date.

Every check can be written into a hash-sealed evidence record. The record refers to a signed, immutable corpus release, and anyone can recompute it in the browser without trusting this site's server logic.

Not legal advice. The consolidated text is not legally authentic; only the Official Journal is binding.
